1. Main objective of the Deservoir system

To let a salesperson prepare a correct, GST-compliant quotation on a mobile phone in a few minutes, and send it to the customer on WhatsApp straight away.

In most small and mid-sized businesses a quotation is still typed into Word or Excel, the GST is worked out by hand, and the file is emailed or photographed and forwarded. That is slow, and mistakes in tax are common. This system brings the whole job into one place: the customer, the items, the prices, the tax and the message, with every figure worked out automatically.

  • Faster quotations — built from saved clients and items rather than typed from scratch.
  • Correct GST every time — the right tax type and rate applied automatically.
  • Sent where customers already are — shared on WhatsApp with a ready-made message.
  • Everything in one view — what has been quoted, what is waiting, what was accepted.

3. Complete functionality

Each feature below describes what this prototype actually shows today. Where something is only illustrated rather than working, it is marked DEMO ONLY.

How the app is organised

+ (centre)Starts a new quotation.

Dashboard HOME TAB

The first screen, giving an at-a-glance view of the business:

  • Quoted this month — total value of quotations raised.
  • Awaiting — quotations that are Sent or Viewed and waiting for a reply.
  • Accepted — value of quotations the customer has accepted this year.
  • Expiring — quotations whose validity runs out within 7 days, so they can be followed up.
  • Clients — number of customers on record.
  • Recent quotations — the latest quotes, each with a share shortcut.
  • GST by slab — how much tax sits in each GST rate.

Quotation management QUOTES TAB

Every quotation is listed with the customer, quotation number, value and status. A row of filter buttons narrows the list to All, Draft, Sent, Viewed, Accepted or Expired. Tapping a quotation opens it; a share button sends it again without opening it first.

StatusMeaning
DraftBeing prepared, not yet sent.
SentShared with the customer.
ViewedThe customer has seen it.
AcceptedThe customer has agreed to it.
ExpiredThe validity date has passed.

In the prototype these statuses come from sample data. How a quotation moves between them — especially "Viewed" — depends on how sharing is built in the final application.

Create new quotation + BUTTON

The quotation builder shows, from top to bottom:

  • Quotation number with its status (Draft), the quote date and the valid until date.
  • Bill to — the selected client, with a Change option.
  • Items — each line shows description, HSN, quantity, unit, rate, discount and GST rate. Lines can be added from the item master, edited or removed.
  • Tax summary — worked out live as lines change.
  • Amount in words, a short tax logic explanation, and the terms.

When a line is edited, quantity can be stepped up and down, discount entered, and the GST rate chosen from the slabs, with a live preview of the line amount before it is saved.

Client management MORE › CLIENTS

A list of customers, each shown with their state and whether they will be charged CGST + SGST or IGST. A new client is added with name, GSTIN, state, address, contact person, email and WhatsApp number (name, email and WhatsApp number are required).

  • GSTIN checking — the GSTIN is converted to capitals and checked against the standard 15-character pattern; a warning appears if it does not match.
  • State from GSTIN — the first two digits fill in the state automatically (for example 24 = Gujarat, 27 = Maharashtra).
  • Unregistered customers — a customer without a GSTIN is shown as URP (Unregistered Person).

Item / product management ITEMS TAB

The item master holds everything that can be quoted: products and services, each with a name, HSN / SAC code, unit (Nos, Set, Job, Year, Trip and so on), rate and GST rate, and marked as Goods or Service. New items can be added from this screen or from inside a quotation. Once saved, an item can be dropped into any quotation with its HSN, unit, price and GST rate already filled in.

HSN & GST management ITEMS TAB

Every item carries its own HSN or SAC code and its own GST rate. This matters because a single quotation often mixes rates — a pump at 18%, a solar heater at 12% and a testing kit at 5%. The system keeps each line's rate separate and never applies one rate to the whole quotation. The GST slabs available are 0%, 5%, 12%, 18% and 28%.

Automatic GST calculation

For each line, and then for the whole quotation:

Line amount = Quantity × Rate Discount = Line amount × Discount % Taxable value = Line amount − Discount GST = Taxable value × GST rate of that line Grand total = Total taxable value + total GST, rounded to the nearest rupee

The difference caused by rounding is shown as a separate Round off line, as GST documents require.

CGST + SGST for intra-state supply

When the customer is in the same state as the company (both in Gujarat, for example), the GST is split into two equal halves: CGST (Central) and SGST (State). 18% GST becomes CGST 9% + SGST 9%.

IGST for inter-state supply

When the customer is in a different state (the company in Gujarat, the customer in Maharashtra), the full rate is charged as IGST (Integrated GST). 18% GST is charged as IGST 18%.

The decision is made automatically by comparing the company's state with the customer's state. Changing the customer on a quotation switches between CGST + SGST and IGST instantly. The total stays the same either way — only the way the tax is split changes. The quotation also shows a sentence explaining which rule was applied and why.

Tax summary

Under the items, the quotation shows:

LineShows
Gross valueQuantity × rate for every line, added up.
DiscountTotal discount given.
Taxable valueThe amount GST is charged on.
CGST / SGST or IGSTListed separately for each GST rate used on the quotation.

Quotation validity

Each quotation has a quote date and a valid until date. The default validity period (in days) is set once in the company profile. Quotations that are about to expire are highlighted on the dashboard, and ones past their date are marked Expired.

Company profile MORE › COMPANY PROFILE

The details printed on every quotation, entered once:

  • Identity — company name, GSTIN and address. The GSTIN's state code decides whether customers are charged CGST + SGST or IGST.
  • Contact — email, WhatsApp number and landline.
  • Bank — bank name, branch, account number and IFSC.
  • Numbering — quotation prefix, next number and default validity.

WhatsApp quotation sharing SHARE BUTTON DEMO ONLY

Tapping share opens a screen that looks like a WhatsApp chat with the customer. It shows the customer's name and number, a PDF attachment named after the quotation, and a ready-written message. A summary lists the contact, number, attachment and total. Send on WhatsApp completes the action.

The message is built from a template that fills itself in:

Namaste {contact}, Please find the quotation from *{company}*. *Quote no:* {no} *Date:* {date} *Valid until:* {valid} *Items:* {items} *Total (incl. GST):* {total} PDF attached. Happy to revise if anything needs a change. — {rep}, {company} {phone}

Words between *asterisks* appear bold in WhatsApp. The template can be edited under More › WhatsApp message.

In the prototype, nothing is actually sent. Pressing Send on WhatsApp shows a confirmation marked "(demo)". The PDF shown is a placeholder — no file is created. Real sending, with a real PDF attached, is built in the final application (see Sections 8 and 9).

Terms & conditions

Standard terms appear at the foot of every quotation. The prototype's sample terms are:

  1. Prices are ex-works Ahmedabad.
  2. Payment: 50% advance, balance before dispatch.
  3. Delivery: 3–4 weeks from confirmed order.
  4. Warranty: 12 months against manufacturing defects.

In the final application these would be set by the company, and could be edited for an individual quotation.

Quote numbering / settings COMPANY PROFILE › NUMBERING

Quotations are numbered in a running series such as QT/2026-27/0042 — a prefix, the financial year and a sequence number. The prefix, the next number and the default validity in days are all set in the company profile.

10. Questions to ask the client before we build

The answers to these decide the scope, the price and the timeline of the final application.

USERS & ACCESS
  1. How many people will use the system, and in what roles (owner, sales, accounts)?
  2. Should everyone see every quotation, or only their own?
  3. Who is allowed to change prices, give discounts or delete a quotation?
  4. Do discounts above a certain level need approval?
DEVICES & WHERE IT RUNS
  1. Will the team use it on mobile phones, a computer in the office, or both?
  2. Must it work without an internet connection?
  3. Should the data stay on the company's own computer, or be kept online so it can be reached from anywhere?
QUOTATION FORMAT
  1. Can you share a quotation you send today, so the new format matches your existing one?
  2. What must appear on every quotation — logo, bank details, signature, stamp?
  3. How should quotations be numbered, and does the series restart each financial year?
  4. How many days is a quotation normally valid for?
  5. Are your terms the same for every customer, or do they change by customer or product?
  6. Do you need a longer description or specification under an item?
PRODUCTS, CUSTOMERS & TAX
  1. Roughly how many products and services do you sell, and can you share the list with HSN codes and GST rates?
  2. Roughly how many customers, and can you share that list?
  3. Do you sell to customers outside Gujarat (so IGST applies)?
  4. Do you have different price lists for different customers?
  5. What units do you sell in (Nos, Kg, Set, Job, metre and so on)?
SHARING
  1. Should quotations be sent on WhatsApp, email, or both?
  2. Should WhatsApp sending be automatic through the WhatsApp Business API, or through the salesperson's own WhatsApp?
  3. Do you want to know when a customer has opened a quotation?
BEYOND QUOTATIONS
  1. Once a quotation is accepted, do you want to turn it into a tax invoice here?
  2. Do you need to track payments received?
  3. Which reports matter most to you?
  4. Do you need stock or inventory tracking?
  5. Should it connect to Tally or any accounting software you already use?
DATA, SUPPORT & TIMELINE
  1. How important is backup, and where should backups be kept?
  2. Do you have old quotations or records that need to be brought across?
  3. Will you need ongoing support and changes after the application goes live?
  4. When do you need the application ready, and is there a budget range in mind?
