01 · Overview

Main objective & why this system is used

What Spirit Engineering is, in one line, and the reason a business runs it.

Main objective. Give a small or mid-sized manufacturing business a single place to run its complete operation — managing its people and money internally, and its machines, enquiries and sales externally — replacing the scattered mix of Excel sheets, WhatsApp chats, paper registers and memory that most such businesses run on today.

Why it is used. Most owner-run manufacturing units keep attendance in one Excel file, salary in another, cash notes in a diary, bills in a billing tool, machine videos in a phone gallery, and customer enquiries in their head or in chat history. Nothing talks to anything else, only the owner knows where things are, and time leaks out of every day. Spirit Engineering brings all of that under one roof so the business runs on a system instead of on the owner's memory.

The suite has two connected sides

Operations (HRMS & Accounts) — the internal engine: workforce, attendance, payroll, cashbook, bills and reports, admin-controlled.  ·  Catalogue & Sales — the outward face: machine catalogue, customer enquiries, quotations with GST, sales and customer records.

05 · Compliance

GST & compliance

GST is built into the places it belongs — billing and quotations — not bolted on.

  • Automatic CGST/SGST split on quotations and bills at the chosen GST rate (e.g. 18% → 9% + 9%).
  • Firm identity on every document — GSTIN, HSN code, delivery and payment terms carried onto quotes and bills.
  • Amount in words and clean totals, so documents look professional and are ready to send.
  • Period GST summary — taxable value, CGST, SGST and tax collected, ready to hand to the accountant/CA.
Note

Spirit Engineering handles GST at the document and reporting level for the business owner. It is not a return-filing portal — the GST summary is designed to make the CA's job easier, and can be extended toward filing in a production build if a client needs it.

06 · Workflow

Complete business workflow

How a normal working day flows through the two sides of the suite.

Mark the muster
Staff and labour attendance is entered for the day — this feeds payroll later.
Send a machine
A customer calls asking for a machine; the owner shares its video and catalogue as a link in two taps.
Log the enquiry
The interest that comes back is recorded in the enquiry register and set for follow-up.
Make the quotation
A GST quotation is built — machine, freight, tax — and sent to the customer on WhatsApp.
Close the sale & bill
On confirmation, a bill is raised with GST and the sale is recorded; the customer's history updates.
Record money movement
Cash in and out, purchases and expenses are entered in the cashbook / money book.
Run payroll & review reports
At month-end, payroll is calculated from attendance, and reports show money in/out, sales vs purchase and GST.

12 · Discovery

Client requirement / discovery questions

Ask these before scoping, to fit the suite to the client's real business.

People & payroll
  • How many employees are monthly, and how many are day-wise labour?
  • How is attendance recorded today, and by whom?
  • Do you need statutory deductions (PF / ESI / PT) on payroll?
  • Do workers need salary slips, and do you give advances?
Money & billing
  • How do you track daily cash in and out right now?
  • Do you raise GST bills, and what GST rates apply?
  • Where is billing done today, and who does it?
  • Do you need purchase and expense tracking as well as sales?
Catalogue & sales
  • How do you share machine videos/photos with customers today?
  • Are your phones/mailboxes running out of storage?
  • How do you track enquiries and follow-ups?
  • Do you make quotations, and how long does each one take?
  • Do you keep any customer history today?
Setup & access
  • Should staff have their own logins, or admin-only?
  • Which language(s) do your people work in?
  • One location or multiple branches?
  • Do you have any existing data (Excel/lists) to bring in?
13 · Sales

How to position & present this to clients

A simple, repeatable way to show the value.

The one-line pitch

"Right now your business runs on Excel, a diary, and WhatsApp — and only in your head. This is one app that runs the whole thing: your people and money on one side, your machines and customers on the other."

Lead with their pain, not our features

  • Open with the storage problem — full phones, heavy video sends — because they feel it daily.
  • Then show the enquiry and quotation flow — money they are leaving on the table.
  • Only then show payroll, cashbook and reports — the "run my business properly" layer.

Suggested demo flow (5 minutes)

Send a machine in two taps
Show the catalogue share — the instant "wow".
Log the enquiry and quote it
Show GST calculated automatically and sent on WhatsApp.
Show the money book & report
Net profit and GST for the period, in one screen.
Switch to Operations
Mark muster, run payroll, show the cashbook — in Gujarati if they prefer.
